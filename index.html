Help & Quick Start

ProgressMint Studios • Plan • Focus • Progress

A simple, connected guide. Highlighted references take you directly where you need to go.

1. Set up once, stay organized

Set your current term and add classes in Academics. Active classes become available throughout Assignments and Major Exams, and can be selected for Study sessions.

2. Manage school & everyday life

Today is your dashboard for Quick Add and Theme. The single Schedule brings together classes, assignments, tests, quizzes, major exams, sports, clubs, work, volunteering and personal commitments.

3. Study, follow through & grow

Track everyday schoolwork in Assignments and high-impact midterms, finals and major exams in Exams. Study can be linked to a class or entered as Other for driving school, certification prep and more. Completed effort earns XP and builds meaningful Progress.

4. Family, reviews & privacy

Preview current updates in Parent / Guardian before opening your email app. Create Term and Year Reviews in Progress. Data stays in this browser profile unless you back it up in Settings; nothing is sent automatically.

Data & privacy

This version uses local browser storage and contains no analytics, ad trackers, external scripts or ProgressMint cloud database. Parent sharing opens the device's email app only when the user chooses it.

Before public deployment, privacy language should be re-verified against the exact hosted build and any services added later.
